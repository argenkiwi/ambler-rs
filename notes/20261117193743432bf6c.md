`ambler check` (spec-nodes) maps a `###` heading to an `Id` variant by concatenating its words verbatim, so `### Check M3U File` becomes `CheckM3UFile`, not `CheckM3uFile`. Since Rust variants follow clippy's camel-case acronym style (`M3u`, `Urls`), spec headings must read `Check M3u File` and `Resolve Urls`, odd as that looks. A node wired under two Ids (m3ud's `SaveM3uFile` and `SaveLocalM3uFile`) needs a `###` section for each Id, not one section describing both. Specs also allow only the `# Program Specifications`, `## Nodes` and `## Shared State` sections.
