m3ud needs two yes/no prompts and two saves with different successors. Rather than near-duplicate nodes, `Confirm::new(question)` takes its prompt at construction and `SaveM3uFile` is stateless; the walk owns two fields of each (sharing one Utils type param) and gives them separate Ids (PromptResolve/PromptDownload, SaveM3uFile/SaveLocalM3uFile) so each wires its own edges. Single-outcome nodes use `type Outcome = ()` like Stop. Confirm treats end of input as No, otherwise a closed stdin would loop forever.
